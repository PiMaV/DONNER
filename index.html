DONNER

      
Browser-native 3D/XR explorer for structured data (WETTER). Not a medical device. Not a Game of Life product — Life is the generator example.

      
Examples

      
    
        
  • Game of Life — generator. Each cube is a cell; Z is generations. Play grows the stack. Loop walks a cut of that tape.
    • 
        
  • Lighter Ignition — event-camera counts of a lighter strike. Sparse XY; Z is time. Loop scrubs the recording.
    • 
        
  • Brain MRI Low — example T1 atlas (ICBM 152), 2× mean-binned. All three axes are space. Loop walks a cut. Not a patient scan. Visitor default for ?src=brain.
    • 
        
  • Brain MRI High — the same atlas at native grid. Larger download. ?src=mni152.
    • 
      

      
Share

      
The address bar follows the example: ?src=ignition, ?src=mni152-low (or ?src=brain), ?src=mni152 (High), ?src=conway. Add &quality=high or low. Unknown src values are ignored. No arbitrary file URLs.
